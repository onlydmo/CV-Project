Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to set up automation?
Most projects take 1–3 weeks depending on complexity. Simple workflows can be completed in a few days, while advanced CRM setups with multiple integrations may take 2–4 weeks.
Do I need technical knowledge to use these automations?
Not at all. I build automations to run quietly in the background. You'll get simple documentation and a video walkthrough so your team knows exactly how to use them.
What platforms do you specialize in?
GoHighLevel, Zapier, n8n, Airtable, Vapi, and HubSpot. These cover CRM, workflow automation, AI chatbots, voice AI, and client management—covering all your core database, pipeline, and AI needs.
Can you integrate with my existing tools?
Yes. I connect calendars, payment systems, forms, CRMs, and email tools. If a tool doesn't have an off-the-shelf integration, I write custom webhook integrations using Zapier or n8n.
What if I need changes after the automation is set up?
I provide ongoing support. If a workflow breaks due to an external API change, or if your process changes, I'll log in and adjust the logic to keep things running.
How much can automation really save me?
Most clients reclaim 10 to 20 hours per week. You'll also see faster lead response times, fewer human entry errors, and a more structured pipeline.
Do you offer team training?
Yes. I run a live walkthrough with you or your team, record it for your reference, and write simple guides so everyone is aligned.

Need something built for your specific process?

If your daily workflows don't fit into a standard template, let's map them out. We'll identify the roadblocks and build custom connections that fit.
